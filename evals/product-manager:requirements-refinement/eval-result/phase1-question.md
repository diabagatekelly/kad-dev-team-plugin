Before a slice gets cut for step 1, I need to hear what you actually want —
from you. The scope map names a rough capability ("transaction retrieval");
that isn't a decided WHAT, and I'm not going to infer one from the map.

Five questions, in one pass:

1. **Who specifically benefits from this piece?** Is this just you tracking
   your own accounts, or does anyone else — a partner, a household — need
   access to the same data?

2. **What are you actually trying to accomplish here, in your own terms?**
   Not "retrieve transactions" as the map phrases it — what does this piece
   need to do for you before you'd call it worth having?

3. **Is there a trade-off you care about between a live account connection
   and something simpler?** A live connection means transactions arrive on
   their own but is the harder and riskier path; a periodic manual export
   and import is far simpler and works with essentially any institution, but
   keeps a manual step in your month. I don't want to assume you'd pick the
   safer option — that's your call to make, not mine to default.

4. **What's the simplest version that would still feel like real progress to
   you?** One account connected, or does this need to cover all of them
   before it counts? And is seeing an uncategorized list enough, or does it
   only count once something is done with those transactions?

5. **Did you consider any other approach and reject it?** If so, what was the
   reason — I want the reasoning recorded, not just the choice, so nobody
   silently re-decides it later.
