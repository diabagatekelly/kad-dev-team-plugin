# Scope Map: Members can only pay fines at the desk (scope-map-002)

## Status
Active

Supersedes scope-map-001: the desk staff asked that members first be able
to see what they owe, since most desk payments start with "how much is it?"

## Steps
1. See outstanding fines — a member sees their own outstanding fines and
   amounts in the portal.
2. Pay a fine online — a member pays one outstanding fine from the portal.
3. Fine notices — remind members of outstanding fines by email.
