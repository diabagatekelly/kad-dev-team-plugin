# Slice 1 (scope-map-002 step 2): A member pays an outstanding fine

## Thin path

A signed-in member picks one outstanding fine in the portal and pays it by
card. The fine then shows as paid.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the member pays a fine and the payment succeeds, the fine shows as
  paid.
- Given the payment is declined, the fine stays outstanding and the member
  is told.
