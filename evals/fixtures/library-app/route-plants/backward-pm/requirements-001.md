# Requirements: scope-map-002 step 2 — Pay a fine online (requirements-001)

## Status
Active

## User
Members with an outstanding fine who use the portal.

## What they need
Pay one outstanding fine by card from the portal; it then shows as paid to
them and to staff.

## Why this, why now
Desk staff spend part of every shift taking small card payments.

## Confirmed in scope
- Paying one outstanding fine by card.
- A declined card leaves the fine outstanding and tells the member.

## Confirmed out, for now
- Paying several fines at once; saved cards.

## Constraints in force
- Card payments go through the existing Stripe setup.
- One developer: nothing that needs a new service to run.
