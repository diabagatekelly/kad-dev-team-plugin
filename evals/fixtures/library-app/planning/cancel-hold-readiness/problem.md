# Problem: Holds can only be cancelled at the desk

## What's happening

Members place holds on checked-out books from the desk, and a member who
changes their mind has to ask staff to remove the hold. Until they do, the
member stays in the queue and the next member waits behind a hold nobody
wants.

## Who it affects

Members with a hold they no longer want, the members queued behind them,
and desk staff.

## Constraints

- One developer maintains the service.
