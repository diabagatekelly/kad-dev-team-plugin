# Accessibility criteria: slice 001, staff cancel a member's hold from the desk screen

Written by `accessibility-engineer` (`accessibility-review`), 2026-10-23,
against `visual-ux/screen-design-001.md`.

## Criteria

- A1: Given the cancelled state or the refused state is shown, when a
  screen reader is in use, then the notice is announced once, as a status,
  without moving focus.
- A2: Given the cancel button, when it is reached with the keyboard, then
  it is operable with Enter and Space and its focus ring meets the design
  system's contrast token.
