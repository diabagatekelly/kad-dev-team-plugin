# Requirements: problem: Members can only pay fines at the desk (requirements-001)

## Status
Active

## What has to be true

- A member can see their own outstanding fines without coming in.
- A member can pay one outstanding fine by card, and it then shows as paid
  to them and to staff.
- A declined card leaves the fine outstanding and tells the member.

## Out of scope

Paying several fines in one payment, saved cards.
