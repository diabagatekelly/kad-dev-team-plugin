# Problem: Members have to come to the desk to keep a book longer

## What's happening

A member who wants to keep a book past its due date has to ask at one of
the two staff desks. Most renewals are routine, and members who cannot
come in during opening hours run up fines on books nobody else is
waiting for.

## Who it affects

Members with a book on loan, and the staff at both desks.

## Constraints

- One developer maintains the service.
- The member portal already shows a member their loans.
