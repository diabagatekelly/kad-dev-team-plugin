# Screen design: the holds desk (screen-design-000)

## Thin path covered
The desk screen staff keep open while they work a title's hold queue: the
title, and a notice for the last action taken on it.

## Layout and composition
One column, 640 px at most. The title as the page heading, then the
notice: a heading, one sentence, a Dismiss button. Examples are invented.

- Hold cancelled: `visual-ux/hold-cancelled.html`. The notice with the
  green edge.
- Hold not cancelled: `visual-ux/hold-not-cancelled.html`. The notice with
  the red edge and the reason as its sentence.

## Interaction flow
A cancel answers with one of the two notices. Dismiss removes it.
