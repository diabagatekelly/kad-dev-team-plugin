# Requirements: problem: I can't see all my household spending in one place (requirements-001)

## Status
Active

## User
The owner of the household's accounts: one person, on their own computer.

## What they need
To connect each of their three banks once and see this month's
transactions from all of them in one list, brought up to date when they
ask.

## Why this, why now
Copying figures by hand takes an evening a month and is stale the next
day. Seeing the list is worth more to them than sorting it: categories
come later.

## Confirmed in scope
- Connecting a bank by logging in at the bank through the vendor's own
  window.
- One list of this month's transactions across the connected banks.
- Bringing the list up to date on request.

## Confirmed out, for now
- Removing or replacing a bank connection. A connection, once made,
  stays; the owner said they will not need to drop a bank this year.
- Categories and budgets.
- More than one person.

## Constraints in force
- Free to run: the vendor's free tier allows a small number of
  connections, and each new connection uses one.
- No bank password reaches the app: the login happens in the vendor's
  window.
- Local only: nothing is hosted.

## Outside systems and lasting things
- The bank connection: one per bank, made through the bank-aggregation
  vendor, kept for as long as the owner uses the app.
