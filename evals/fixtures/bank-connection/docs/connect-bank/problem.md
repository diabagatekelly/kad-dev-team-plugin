# Problem: I can't see all my household spending in one place

## What's happening

My household's money is spread over three banks. To see what we spent in
a month I log in to each bank's site and copy figures into a sheet by
hand, which takes an evening and is out of date the next day.

## Who it affects

Me: one person, on my own computer. Nobody else uses it.

## Constraints

- Free to run: a bank-aggregation vendor's free tier, nothing paid.
- The app never sees or stores a bank password.
- It runs on my computer only; nothing is hosted.
