# bank-connection

An empty repository holding only a problem and its agreed requirements
under `docs/connect-bank/`, before anything is sliced: a household money
app that connects bank accounts through a bank-aggregation vendor. The
requirements name one lasting thing, the bank connection, and put
removing or replacing a connection out of scope, so one of its states
(the bank or the vendor ends the connection for good) has no way out in
the app. For `domain-model` and the orchestrator's row for it. Invented;
no real project's content.
