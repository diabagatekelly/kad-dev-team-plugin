Before running a threat pass, I need to establish what's actually in play — I can't derive this from the slice itself:

1. What sensitive data does this slice touch — not a generic list, what this system actually handles?
2. Who are the users, and do they see each other's data?
3. Are there obligations beyond "don't get breached" — a regulatory regime, a contractual requirement, a third party's terms of service?
4. What already exists in this app to build on — an established way of handling secrets or authenticating, if any?
